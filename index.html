Data Deletion Instructions

Meta / Facebook Platform requirement: In compliance with Meta's Platform Terms, users may request the deletion of all data that [App Name] has received through the Meta login integration.

How to request data deletion

You have two options to submit a data deletion request:

Option 1 – Via Facebook Settings
  1. Go to your Facebook Account Settings.
  2. Click Security and Login.
  3. Under Apps and Websites, find [App Name].
  4. Click Remove and then confirm.
  5. This will immediately revoke access and trigger the deletion of any data we hold that was obtained through the Meta login.
Option 2 – Via Email Request
  1. Send an email to [contact@example.com] with the subject line: "Data Deletion Request – [App Name]".
  2. Include the name or email address associated with your account.
  3. We will process your request within 30 days and send a confirmation once completed.

What data will be deleted?

  • Your user profile information (name, email, profile picture) obtained via Meta login.
  • Any activity logs or records associated with your account.
  • All stored permissions and tokens granted through the Meta integration.
Please note: We may retain certain data for a limited period to fulfil legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements, as permitted by applicable law.
